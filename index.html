Clearbill

Aevum Builder Challenge #1 — stablecoin invoicing with mandatory Confirmation of Payee (CoP) on QDay Aevum Testnet (44005), using official tUSD8.

  • A payee issues an invoice bound immutably to official tUSD8.
  • The payer must confirm the payee (on-chain CoP match) before any tUSD8 can move.
  • After payment, an escrow review window allows immediate payer release, deterministic auto-finalization, or a voluntary payee refund.
